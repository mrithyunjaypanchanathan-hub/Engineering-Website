AEROSPACE / LEARNING PATHWAY

Understand flight. Explore space.

Explore how aerodynamic forces, propulsion, and control work together in aircraft and spacecraft.

Topics to explore

  1. 01
    Aerodynamics

    Lift, drag, angle of attack, and how airfoil geometry affects performance.

  2. 02
    Flight mechanics

    Forces in flight, aircraft stability, center of gravity, and control surfaces.

  3. 03
    Space systems

    Orbital motion, spacecraft subsystems, and the tradeoffs in a mission design.

What you can learn to do

  • Explain the forces acting on an aircraft.
  • Compare wing designs using evidence.
  • Describe the basic elements of a space mission.

Tools & methods

Sketching, introductory CAD, and flight simulation concepts

Starting point: no prior experience required
SUGGESTED PRACTICAL EXERCISE

Compare two model wing shapes, record their behavior, and explain how geometry changes the result.
